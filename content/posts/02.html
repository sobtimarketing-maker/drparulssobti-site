Dr Parul Sharma Sobti has been helping people smile with confidence since 2009, with more than 500 successful smile designs along the way. She writes and reviews this series, so it is time you met her.

Where it began

Dr Parul earned her Bachelor of Dental Surgery at SR Dental College, under Maharshi Dayanand University, Rohtak. She is a registered dental surgeon and a member of the Indian Dental Association. Since she began practising in 2009 her work has moved steadily towards cosmetic dentistry, and today she practises as a Digital Smile Designer at Dr Sobti's Dental Care in Rajouri Garden, New Delhi.

Your natural beauty begins with a confident smile.

Why smiles

For Dr Parul, aesthetic dentistry is about people more than teeth. A patient who has covered their mouth in every photograph for years. Someone getting married who wants to stop worrying about one dark tooth. A parent who wants their child's first dental visit to be a good one. A good smile changes how people speak, laugh and carry themselves.

How she works

  • Listen first. Every plan starts with what the patient wants to change and what they want to keep. Patients often say she gives them time and really listens to the problem.
  • Comfort throughout. As one patient put it, she makes sure you are comfortable while getting the treatment. That goes for children too.
  • Healthy before beautiful. Gums and teeth are treated before any cosmetic work begins.
  • Least invasive option first. Whitening, bonding and contouring come before veneers or crowns, wherever they can do the job.
  • Show before doing. With Digital Smile Design, patients see their new smile on screen, and often as a trial in their own mouth, before anything permanent is done.

Working alongside Dr Pushkar Sobti

Dr Parul runs the clinic with Dr Pushkar Sobti, an implantologist with 18 years of experience and more than 5,000 implants placed, who specialises in digital implantology and full-mouth rehabilitation. Between them, a single patient can have a smile designed, missing teeth replaced with guided implants, and the final teeth made to match, all under one roof and all planned digitally from the same 3D scans.
